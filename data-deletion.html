Data Deletion Request

AuraFit Technologies · Last updated: 15 November 2026

If you have messaged AuraFit Technologies on WhatsApp or shared your details through our website, you can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to support@aurafittechnologies.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the phone number you used to contact us on WhatsApp, and your name if you shared it.
  3. You can also send the same request as a WhatsApp message to +91 93564 47844.

What happens next

We will confirm your request and delete the information we hold about you, including your phone number, name, city and the messages you sent us, except anything we must keep to meet legal or accounting requirements. We aim to complete this within 30 days.

Messages held by WhatsApp

Copies of messages stored by WhatsApp or Meta are governed by their own policies. You can manage those in your WhatsApp settings.

See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle information.
