Privacy Policy

AuraFit Technologies · Last updated: 15 November 2026

AuraFit Technologies (“AuraFit”, “we”, “us”) sells electrical products such as lighting, wires and fans. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us on WhatsApp or through our website (www.aurafittechnologies.com), how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • WhatsApp details: your phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Your messages: the text you send us and any product, quantity, city or contact details you choose to share.
  • Enquiry details: for example your name, city, business name and the products you ask about.

We do not ask for payment card numbers, passwords or government ID numbers on WhatsApp. Please do not send them.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and send product specifications, photos and brochures.
  • To prepare quotations and follow up on your enquiry, dealership or bulk order request.
  • To improve our product information and customer service.

3. Automated replies

Our WhatsApp number uses an automated assistant to answer common product questions. Questions it cannot answer may be passed to our team, who may contact you.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. Your messages pass through WhatsApp and Meta Platforms, which provide the messaging service, and through the hosting provider that runs our automated assistant. These providers process information for us under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also share information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and our business records, and then delete or anonymise it.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You can stop messaging us at any time. To request deletion, see our data deletion page.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
